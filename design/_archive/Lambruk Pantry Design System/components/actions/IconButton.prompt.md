Round icon-only action for header utilities (search, account, cart) and card affordances.

```jsx
<IconButton label="Cart" icon={<i data-lucide="shopping-bag" />} />
<IconButton label="Menu" variant="onDark" icon={<i data-lucide="menu" />} />
```

Variants: `ghost` (default, warm tint on hover) · `outline` (hairline border) · `onDark` (for navy bands). Sizes 32/40/48px. Icons are Lucide at 1.5px stroke.
