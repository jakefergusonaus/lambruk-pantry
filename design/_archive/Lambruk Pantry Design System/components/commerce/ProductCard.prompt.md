The shop tile — used in the seasonal rail and category listings.

```jsx
<ProductCard image="/assets/images/product-marmalade.png" category="Preserves"
  name="Blood Orange & Rosemary Marmalade" price="$18.00" onAdd={add} />
```

Prices are pre-formatted strings with the dollar sign. Names are set in Instrument Serif and may wrap to two lines. Omit `onAdd` for a display-only card.
