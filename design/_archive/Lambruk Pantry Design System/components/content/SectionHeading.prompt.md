Opens a page section. Pair a short gold eyebrow with a sentence-case serif title.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="New Releases" title="The Seasonal Spotlight"
  action={<Button variant="ghost">Shop all</Button>} />
```

`align="center"` for statement sections; `tone="dark"` on navy bands; `level={3}` steps the title down to 40px.
