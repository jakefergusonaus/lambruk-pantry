Our Story

From a family kitchen in the Northern Rivers

Lambruk Pantry makes the food we grew up eating: relishes on the breakfast table, sauces for the Sunday roast, and a pot of tea for whoever came through the door.

Meet Paige

A home cook who kept going

Paige Brierley started Lambruk Pantry as a passionate home cook, preserving the family recipes she had grown up with. What began as jars for friends and markets became a small-batch production kitchen supplying country stores and hospitality venues across the region.

Every recipe is still tested the same way: cooked, tasted, adjusted, and only released when it holds up beside the version her family remembers.

In 2026 that work opens a front door, with the first Lambruk Pantry cafe in Ballina.

Straight from the farm

We buy from Australian growers we know, and cook what is in season rather than what is cheapest.

Made with real fruit

Real fruit and vegetables carry the flavour, so we can use less sugar and no artificial sweeteners.

Small batches, tested

Nothing leaves the kitchen until it has been made, tasted and remade enough times to be worth keeping.

Taste the range, or bring it into your own venue.

Customer Care

Shipping, returns and terms

Everything you need to know about how your order reaches you, and what happens if something is not right.

Shipping Refunds Terms

Shipping

Each new order will be shipped in the timeliest manner possible. In-stock items will be shipped within 2-3 business days.

We use Australia Post to ship our products and unfortunately we cannot control shipping times. We do not ship on weekends or Public Holidays. Currently, we can only ship Australia-wide.

Shipping Rates

Australia — Express Shipping
Products$14.00
Hampers$16.00
New Zealand — Express Shipping
Products and Hampers$25.00

We do not ship any order on a Friday, Saturday, Sunday and Public Holidays.

We will be closed from the 20th December to 20th January and 21st June - 5 July, orders placed in this time will be shipped / delivered once we return to work.

Returns & Refunds

If you receive a product that is damaged, not what you had ordered or a mistake has been made, we are happy to exchange the purchase within 7 days of receiving it. Unfortunately we do not offer refunds, instead are happy to exchange or issue a store credit.

Please email us at sales@lambrukpantry.com

Terms & Conditions

Cancelling an Order

Lambruk Pantry does not allow the cancellation of an order once the order is complete.

Damaged Items

We always try aim to ensure the quality of our products are uncompromised. However, in the rare occurrence that products break during transit or are missed during quality checks, we'll take care of it quickly and hassle-free!

Please send us an email at sales@lambrukpantry.com and follow the following steps:

Include your Order Number in the subject of the email. Please include your Name, Email Address and Product details in the contents of the email. Attach photos of the damaged product you received to the email.

Gift Cards

Lambruk Gift Cards are only to be used at Lambruk Pantry. Gift Cards are non-refundable and non-returnable. They never expire. Both physical and e-Gift Cards are available. Shipping costs will be charged on physical Gift Card purchases.

Payment

Lambruk Pantry takes payment upon checkout, not when an item has been shipped. Your payment is processed securely through our secure payment systems.

Discount Codes & Promotions

Lambruk occasionally offers discount codes for promotions and sales, which can be redeemed at checkout. Can not be used with any other offer.
