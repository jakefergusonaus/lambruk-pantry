# Phase 10 — Preview and Handoff

## Development preview

Use a development theme during active work. Record its temporary nature and preview/editor URLs without exposing credentials.

## Durable client review

When requested:

1. Reconfirm store domain and target name.
2. Create or update a specifically identified unpublished theme.
3. Verify its status remains unpublished.
4. Run smoke checks against its durable preview.
5. Provide preview/editor links, theme ID/name/status, changed files, checks, editor guidance and known deviations.

Stop before publishing. Publishing is a distinct external change requiring a fresh explicit request that names the exact approved unpublished theme and store.

Complete the current state, preservation and QA artefacts without copying a
historical activity log into them. Replace stale facts and mark superseded
decisions clearly.
