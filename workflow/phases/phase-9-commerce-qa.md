# Phase 9 — Commerce, Accessibility, Performance and SEO QA

Use representative real catalogue data without mutating operational data.

## Commerce

- Single/multi-variant, available, sold-out, discounted and missing-media products
- Variant/price/media/availability updates
- Quantity, add-to-cart, cart edits, errors and checkout handoff
- Collection filters, sorting, pagination and empty states
- Search, predictive search, menus and localization
- Selling plans, pickup, Markets, accounts, apps and custom data when present

## Accessibility

- Landmarks and headings
- Keyboard order and operability
- Visible focus and reduced motion
- Accessible names, form labels, errors and live announcements
- Dialog/menu disclosure behavior and contrast

## Performance and SEO

- Responsive images, dimensions, lazy/eager loading and media formats
- Parser/render-blocking assets, duplicate code and expensive Liquid loops
- Canonicals, titles, descriptions, structured data, headings, alt text and indexable HTML

Record exact routes, products/collections used, outcomes and remaining risks in `QA_REPORT.md`.
